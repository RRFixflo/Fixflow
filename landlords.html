Renters’ Rights Act 2025

The new rules — handled for you

Letting in England changed on 1 May 2026. We keep your tenancies compliant, so you don’t have to keep up with every detail.

🔁

Periodic tenancies

All tenancies are now rolling assured periodic tenancies — no fixed terms and no section 21. We set them up correctly from day one.

⚖️

Possession, done properly

Tenancies can only be ended on the legal grounds — for example selling, moving in or rent arrears. We’ll advise you on the right route and notice.

📈

Rent reviews

Rent can go up once a year using the formal notice. We track the dates and prepare the notice for you.

🏷️

Fair advertising

Every property is advertised at a set rent, with no bidding, and no more than one month’s rent taken in advance.

🐾

Pet requests

Tenants can ask to keep a pet. We handle the request and the reply within the legal time limit, with your say.

🛡️

What’s coming next

We’ll guide you through the new landlord database, ombudsman and Decent Homes Standard as they come in.
