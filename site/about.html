About us

A modern agency, built around people.

Residential Realtors is a London letting and property management agency with a decade of experience in the property industry.

Who we are

Doing estate agency properly

We’re a team of property professionals bringing landlords and tenants together across London — homes for professionals, couples, sharers and families alike.

We think estate agency has been left behind: slow processes, poor communication and too little use of the technology that makes life easier. So we built our own system to run every tenancy — from online offers and referencing to repairs, rent and statements — so nothing slips and everyone knows what’s happening.

But our name is only as good as our people. Listening to what landlords and tenants actually need, and building a real relationship with each of them, is what lets us give the right answers — and keep the trust that renting depends on.
