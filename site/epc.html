What the law says

  • Needed before a home is marketed for let or sale; give a copy to tenants
  • Valid for 10 years
  • Rented homes must be rated E or better, unless an exemption is registered
  • Fines of up to £5,000 for letting a home below the minimum standard
Energy Performance of Buildings (England and Wales) Regulations 2012 and the private rented sector minimum standard

Questions landlords ask

How long does an EPC visit take?

Usually 30 to 60 minutes for a typical flat or house.

How long is an EPC valid?

10 years — you can check yours with our free EPC checker.

What if my rating is F or G?

You can’t start a new let until it’s E or above, or an exemption is registered. The EPC lists the improvements that would raise it.

Do I need to be there?

No. We can arrange access with your tenant directly, or you can leave keys with us.
