Questions landlords ask

Is it really free?

Yes — our help with the application is free. The council charges its own licence fee, which you pay to them. Our licence checker shows each London council’s fees.

How do I know if I need a licence?

It depends on the council, the ward and who lives in the property. Use our free licence checker, or ask us and we’ll check for you.

What if I’m missing a certificate?

We can book your Gas Safety certificate, EICR or EPC at our fixed prices so the application isn’t held up.

How long does a licence last?

Usually up to five years, depending on the council’s scheme.
