How renting works

From offer to keys

Here’s what happens once you’ve found the right place.

Make an offer

Send your offer online. Once it’s agreed, you pay a holding deposit of up to one week’s rent to secure the property.

Referencing

You and any guarantor will get an email from Let Alliance, our referencing partner — complete it as soon as you can.

Sign & pay

Sign your tenancy agreement online, then pay your first month’s rent and deposit (minus the holding deposit).

Move in

Collect your keys, check the inventory and settle in. Your deposit is protected in a government-approved scheme.

Tenant fees

No hidden fees

Under the Tenant Fees Act 2019 we never charge admin, referencing or check-in fees. The only payments we can ask for are:

  • Rent
  • A refundable holding deposit — no more than 1 week’s rent
  • A refundable tenancy deposit — no more than 5 weeks’ rent (6 weeks if the yearly rent is £50,000 or more)
  • Payments allowed by law and set out in your tenancy agreement, e.g. for a change you ask for, a lost key, or rent more than 14 days late
  • Your own bills: utilities, council tax, TV licence and internet

Questions

How do I report a repair?

Use our online repair form any time — describe the problem, add photos and say when we can visit. You’ll get a reference and updates as it’s fixed.

What happens to my holding deposit?

It secures the property while referencing is done. When the tenancy goes ahead it goes towards your first rent and deposit. If the landlord decides not to go ahead, it’s refunded.

Who are Let Alliance?

Our referencing partner. Once your offer is accepted, you and any guarantor will receive an email from them — please complete it quickly, as we can’t progress the tenancy until all references are done.

Where is my deposit kept?

Your deposit is registered with a government-approved tenancy deposit protection scheme, and you’ll receive the details after you move in.

How do I pay my rent?

By standing order to the account in your tenancy agreement, so it arrives on the due date each month.

Something urgent out of hours?

Report it online straight away. For a gas leak call 0800 111 999; for fire or danger to life call 999.
