What the law says

🔥

Gas Safety — every year

Landlords must have every gas appliance, fitting and flue checked by a Gas Safe registered engineer every 12 months, and give the tenant a copy of the record within 28 days of the check (and new tenants before they move in).

Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998
⚡

Electrics — at least every 5 years

Landlords in England must have the electrical installation inspected and tested by a qualified person at least every five years, give tenants a copy of the report within 28 days, and fix anything the report says must be put right.

Electrical Safety Standards in the Private Rented Sector (England) Regulations 2020
🏷️

EPC — every 10 years

A home needs a valid Energy Performance Certificate before it’s marketed for let or sale, and the tenant must be given a copy. Rented homes must be rated E or better unless an exemption is registered.

Energy Performance of Buildings Regulations 2012 and the private rented sector minimum standard
