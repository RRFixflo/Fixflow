What the law says

  • Check every 12 months by a Gas Safe registered engineer
  • Give your tenant a copy within 28 days of the check, and new tenants before they move in
  • Keep each record for at least two years
  • Not having one is a criminal offence and can lead to large fines or prison
Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998

Questions landlords ask

How long does a Gas Safety check take?

Usually 30 to 60 minutes, depending on the number of appliances.

Do I need to be there?

No. Choose "Contact my tenant directly" when you book and we’ll arrange a time with your tenant, or leave keys with us.

When will I get the certificate?

We email it to you once the visit is done, ready to give to your tenant.

What if an appliance fails?

The engineer will explain what’s needed and we’ll send you a quote for the repair. An unsafe appliance must not be used until it’s fixed.
