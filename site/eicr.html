What the law says

  • Inspect and test at least every five years by a qualified person
  • Give tenants a copy within 28 days, and new tenants before they move in
  • Fix anything coded C1, C2 or FI within 28 days (or sooner if the report says so)
  • Councils can fine landlords up to £30,000 for breaches
Electrical Safety Standards in the Private Rented Sector (England) Regulations 2020

Questions landlords ask

How long does an EICR take?

Usually two to four hours, depending on the size of the property. The power will be off for short periods.

What do C1, C2 and C3 mean?

C1 is danger present, C2 is potentially dangerous — both need fixing. C3 is an improvement recommended. FI means further investigation is needed.

Do I need to be there?

No. We can arrange access with your tenant directly, or you can leave keys with us.

What if the report is unsatisfactory?

We’ll send you a quote for the remedial work and a new certificate once it’s done.
