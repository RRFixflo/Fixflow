Good to know

Tax on UK rent if you live abroad

If you live outside the UK for more than six months a year, the UK’s Non-Resident Landlord Scheme applies. Unless HMRC approves you to receive rent with no tax taken off, your letting agent (or your tenant, if there’s no agent) must deduct basic-rate tax from the rent and pay it to HMRC. You can apply to HMRC to receive your rent gross.

This is general information, not tax advice. Read HMRC’s guidance on GOV.UK and speak to an accountant about your situation.
