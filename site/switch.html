How switching works

Changing letting agent, step by step

  1. Check your current agreementLook for the notice period, any fee for ending early, and whether the agent can keep charging a fee for a tenant they found. Every contract is different.
  2. Give notice in writingSend notice by email or letter, following what your agreement says, and keep a copy.
  3. Arrange the handoverThe new agent collects the tenancy agreement, certificates, inventory, keys and rent records. If the old agent protected the deposit, it has to stay protected and be moved or re-registered properly.
  4. Tell your tenantYour tenant’s tenancy carries on as normal; they just need the new contact details and where to pay the rent.

Common questions

Can I change letting agent during a tenancy?

Usually, yes. It depends on the notice period and terms in your agreement with your current agent. The tenancy itself is between you and your tenant, so it carries on.

Will my tenant have to move out?

No. Changing agent doesn’t end the tenancy. Your tenant stays, and we take over the day-to-day running.

What happens to the deposit?

It must stay protected in a government-approved scheme the whole time. If your old agent protected it, it’s transferred or re-protected and your tenant is told.

Will I have to pay my old agent anything?

Some agreements charge a fee for ending early or keep a fee for a tenant they introduced. Check your agreement. We’re happy to look through it with you.
