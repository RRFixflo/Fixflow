Questions landlords ask

Is it really free?

Yes. There’s no charge and no obligation to let your property with us.

Will my lender accept it?

Buy-to-let lenders and brokers usually ask for a letting agent’s rental valuation to check the rent covers the mortgage. Each lender has its own rules, so check with your broker what they need — we’re happy to add the lender’s name or reference to the letter.

Is this the same as a mortgage valuation survey?

No. Your lender arranges its own valuation of what the property is worth, by a RICS surveyor. Our letter covers the rent the property should achieve, which is what lenders ask letting agents for.

How quickly will I get it?

Usually within one working day of hearing from you. If your broker needs it by a certain date, tell us on the form.

What if the property is empty or I haven’t bought it yet?

That’s fine. We base the figure on similar homes let or advertised nearby.
